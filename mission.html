🌱 OUR NOBLE PURPOSE

Combating the Sachet Culture

The Philippines stands at a critical environmental crossroads. Millions of single-use plastic sachets are discarded daily, clogging waterways and threatening marine life. RefillMap PH was created to map out and scale accessible refilling infrastructure so sustainable living is reachable for every Filipino.

How Our Directory Operates

We prioritize accurate, reliable data. Here is how our volunteer networks find, audit, and list community zero-waste stations on the map.

⌕

1. Community Sourcing

Conscious buyers and micro-refillers submit spot suggestions through our online portal, detailing locations and inventory.

⌾

2. On-Site Audits

Our regional volunteer champions visit suggested stores in person, checking sanitation standards, scale calibration, and safety.

♧

3. Lifetime Listing

Verified stations receive a free lifetime directory profile and eco-signage, boosting foot traffic from local residents.
